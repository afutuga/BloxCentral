Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-20

This Privacy Policy explains what data BloxCentral ("the Service") processes and how, in connection with publishing short-form gaming video content to connected social media accounts.

1. Data we process

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens for platforms you connect (e.g. YouTube, TikTok, Instagram), used only to publish content to that account.
  • Basic account identifiers returned by the platform's API (e.g. channel ID, username) needed to address the correct account.
  • The video files, titles, descriptions, and hashtags generated by the Service for publishing.

2. How data is used

Data is used exclusively to publish content to the accounts you have explicitly connected. Tokens are stored to allow scheduled and automated publishing without requiring you to log in each time.

3. Data we do not do

We do not sell or share your data with third parties. We do not use your data for advertising. Data is not accessed by anyone other than the operator of the Service.

4. Storage and retention

Tokens and account data are stored in the Service's private database and object storage until you disconnect the account or request deletion. Disconnecting a platform in the Service revokes local access to the stored token.

5. Your rights

You may revoke the Service's access at any time from the connected platform's account settings (this immediately invalidates the token), or request deletion of any data we hold by contacting us below.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy or data deletion requests: bayektuga.ds@gmail.com
